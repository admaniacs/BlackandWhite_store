1B/W — direction & atomic explorations
Brief & assumptions

Affordable streetwear basics, nothing but black & white

Palette
Ink #000, paper #fff, four greys. No colour anywhere — contrast and inversion do the work an accent normally does.
Type
Archivo Black for headlines (uppercase, tight, oversized), Archivo for UI, monospace for meta/specs.
Rules
Zero border radius, 1px hairlines, hard edges, no shadows inside the store UI, no emoji, no gradients.
Voice
Bold and loud — short imperative lines, all caps where it earns it. Prices plain, no "from $".
Catalogue
Hoodies, tees, shirts, shoes, hats, watches. Watches sit as a peer collection, not a premium sub-brand.
Imagery
Studio shots on white. Every slot below is a labelled placeholder — drop your real crops in and nothing else moves.
